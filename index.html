A place to make technology feel possible

BEYCAN is a learning space where curiosity leads. Students explore robotics, code, artificial intelligence and design through hands-on projects, guided by Beybot, our friendly companion for the journey.

Every topic starts with something you can touch, test or build. Ideas turn into working prototypes, and prototypes turn into confidence.

Explore our learning universe

Four worlds, one connected path. Start anywhere and move between them as your interests grow.

Robotics and IoT

Build machines that sense and move.

Robotics and IoT

Connect sensors, motors and microcontrollers, then watch your code act in the physical world.

  • Circuits, sensors and actuators
  • Programming microcontrollers
  • Connected devices and smart-home ideas
  • Build-and-test robotics challenges

Coding and Web Development

Learn how software is structured, from your first program to pages people can open in a browser.

  • Programming fundamentals and problem solving
  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Building interactive web projects
  • Debugging and clean-code habits

Artificial Intelligence and Data

Understand how machines learn from data, and how to use that power thoughtfully.

  • Working with and visualizing data
  • Introductory machine learning
  • Using AI tools responsibly
  • Projects that answer real questions

Design and Animation

Combine creativity with technique to make visuals, interfaces and stories that move.

  • Design principles and visual storytelling
  • 2D and 3D animation basics
  • Interface and experience design
  • Turning sketches into finished work

Workshops and student projects

Learning sticks when you make something. Workshops are short and focused; projects go further and end with something you can show.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions that introduce a tool or idea and finish with a small working result.

Student projects

Longer builds where learners plan, prototype, test and present their own ideas.
